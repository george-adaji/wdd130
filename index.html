Nigeria

Nigeria is a country in West Africa and the most populous nation on the continent. It is bordered by Benin to the west, Niger to the north, Chad and Cameroon to the east, and the Gulf of Guinea to the south. Abuja is the capital city, while Lagos is the largest city. The country is known for its diverse cultures, music, film, and food.
