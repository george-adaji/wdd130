Gurara Rapids Rafting

Our mission is to share the thrill of the river safely, responsibly, and with a smile. Every trip is led by trained guides who love the water.

History

Gurara Rapids Rafting began with a small group of friends and one inflatable raft. Over the years we grew into a full team of guides, offering trips for beginners and experienced paddlers alike.
